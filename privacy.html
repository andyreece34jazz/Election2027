Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

About this service

Nigerian Election 2027 is an independent public information and election-evidence tracking project. It is not an official government or INEC service.

Information submitted through WhatsApp

Users may submit photographs of polling-unit result documents through WhatsApp for evidence collection and verification. Submitted images may be stored securely and processed for verification and aggregation.

How information is used

Submitted evidence is used to identify polling-unit results, verify submitted information and produce aggregated public information. Evidence images and internal verification information are not displayed on the public results pages.

Information sharing

Submitted evidence is not sold. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the submission, storage and verification infrastructure.

Security

Access to submitted evidence and internal verification data is restricted to authorised systems and personnel.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning submitted information, please contact the project operator through the contact details provided on the website.
