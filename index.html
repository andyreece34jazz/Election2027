How to Submit Your Polling Unit Result

1. Snap Clearly

Take a clear, well-lit photo of the entire EC8A form. Make sure all numbers and text are readable. Do not send it as a document.

2. Include Your PU Code

Send the image via WhatsApp and type your 9-digit Polling Unit code as the caption (e.g., 29/13/06/009).

3. Await Review

Our system extracts the figures, and a human reviewer checks them. Submissions do not immediately change public figures.

Methodology & FAQ

What does "Estimated Figures" mean?

We do not claim to replace official INEC declarations. Our platform aggregates independently verified polling unit results to provide a parallel, transparent estimate of the election outcome.

What is "Matched vs. Single Source"?

Matched: Two or more independent submissions from the same polling unit confirm the same numbers.
Single Source: Only one submission has been verified. We publish this as a provisional data point.

Data Privacy

Your phone number and personal details are never published. The public views only display aggregated polling unit figures, matched source counts, and coverage percentages.

How to report an error

If you spot an inaccuracy, please use the Contact/Privacy page or message the WhatsApp number with the word CORRECTION.
