Methodology & FAQ

What does "Estimated Figures" mean?

We do not claim to replace official INEC declarations. Our platform aggregates independently verified polling unit results to provide a parallel, transparent estimate of the election outcome.

How is coverage calculated?

Coverage represents the number of verified polling units divided by the total number of registered polling units for that region. Estimates become more reliable as coverage increases.
