Methodology & FAQs

1. Is this an official INEC website?
No. This is an independent, non-partisan public evidence platform. We are not affiliated with INEC, any political party, or government body. We do not replace or represent the official electoral process.
2. Are these official election results?
No. All figures displayed are estimates based on polling-unit evidence submitted to and approved by our platform. They are meant to provide transparency and may be incomplete. They should not be treated as official results.
3. How do I submit my polling-unit results?

It only takes a minute:

  1. Take a sharp, clear photograph of the completed EC8A result form after the count.
  2. Send it through our designated WhatsApp channel.
  3. Include your polling-unit code as the caption (e.g., 26/10/01/007). If you don't know your code, use our polling-unit search tool.
4. Why are some polling units or party figures missing?

A dash (—) means no reportable figure is currently available; it does not mean zero. Data may be missing because:

  • Evidence has not yet been submitted.
  • The photo was unclear or incomplete.
  • The submission is still undergoing manual review.
5. How does the verification process work?
Every submission is manually reviewed by our team to ensure the photo matches the correct polling unit and the numbers are legible. We cross-reference the data before it is approved and added to the public estimates.
6. How often is the dashboard updated?
The dashboard updates dynamically as new evidence is reviewed and approved by our team. During peak submission times, there may be a slight delay.
7. How is my personal information protected?
We prioritize the privacy of our submitters. Access to private submission details is strictly restricted. Please read our Privacy Policy to understand what data we collect, how it is used, and how to raise a privacy concern.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Protect Your Vote ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our platform. We are committed to complying with data protection laws, including the GDPR.

1. Data We Collect

We are a civic evidence platform. We only collect the minimum data required to verify election results:

  • Submission Data: Photographs of EC8A forms and polling unit codes.
  • Communications: Your WhatsApp number (for submitting evidence).
  • Technical Data: Essential cookies/local storage to manage site preferences.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data solely to verify, aggregate, and display polling unit results on our public dashboard. We do not sell your data to third parties, nor do we use it for targeted advertising.

3. Data Retention and Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your submissions. All evidence is reviewed manually. To protect the privacy of our citizen volunteers, we anonymize submitter information before publishing dashboard figures.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (or other jurisdictions with similar laws), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data (e.g., your WhatsApp number).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@protectyourvote.org. We will respond within 30 days.
